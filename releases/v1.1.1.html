RELEASE NOTES

Release 1.1.1

Build 51 · Stream Deck plugin 0.2.5 · API v1

New and fixed in 1.1.1

Mac face tracking now runs independently of the preview display. Select the camera source and turn Tracking On; Preview can stay off, and tracking can continue while the window is minimized or hidden. Preview On/Off shares the camera feed. Turning both off releases capture. A successful On preset recall can acquire tracking frames without enabling the preview display.

The horizontal Speed slider has four levels: 1 Slow (16°/s), 2 Medium (24°/s), 3 Fast (32°/s) and 4 Very Fast (38°/s). New settings default to level 3; existing preferences and preset speeds are preserved. Higher levels require a compatible reviewed motion profile; select level 1 or 2 when the current profile does not permit them. These are requested pacing limits, not measured motor speeds. Very Fast uses a separate experimental native profile with its own bounds.

Tracking uses the current app Speed as its ceiling and accepts speed changes while following. Proportional response slows movement near the centre. A preset’s saved speed controls its transition; tracking afterward uses the current app Speed. The face outline now aligns with the displayed image, including resized or letterboxed previews, with margins for the forehead and chin.

Losing the face immediately pauses steering and hides the outline. Tracking can recover the nearby associated face for three seconds, with a visible countdown; it turns Off if recovery expires. It does not scan or switch to a distant face. Manual movement, Escape, source changes, disconnect and faults still take priority. The existing continuous execution budget remains.

Temporary video interruptions get bounded AVFoundation recovery. Video delivery and face detection use separate queues; detection analyzes a smaller full-image buffer without changing capture/display quality. Stale inference pauses steering; only fresh detections can resume it. Persistent camera/detector loss stops tracking. Reconnect leaves preview and tracking off.

Held movement at a travel limit refreshes control receipts before expiry. Exact correlated native pre-SET range refusals retain the read/Stop path and a specific error cause. Real USB, protocol and uncertain-write failures stop control without write retries. Tracking status identifies travel limits and exposes the full message as a tooltip.

Presets retain tracking On/Off, framing and speed choices. Settings, camera limits and Stream Deck pairing are preserved; plugin 0.2.5 remains compatible. Face processing stays local; no images or face positions are saved or uploaded. Tracking On uses camera access even with Preview off.

The owner reviewed the latest build on one Mac with direct Pocket USB before approving release. This is qualitative acceptance, not measured 38°/s movement, sustained full-window following, physical stop, image-space accuracy or extended continuity qualification. Zoom/portrait variants and OBS tracking remain unqualified. A previous return-recovery trial exceeded its temporary clearance through the existing broader recovery envelope; that confinement check remains failed. Native DJI ActiveTrack must remain off.

USB controls and preview

Control Pocket 4 pan, tilt and zoom without OBS. Enable Pocket camera · USB for direct local preview, or choose optional OBS Virtual Camera when using OBS. Preview starts explicitly. Disconnect stops capture and clears the image; reconnect leaves it off. Osmo Webcam does not record or upload preview video.

Presets and motion

Save twenty presets per camera with the first Add Preset card. Each preset can choose a transition and separate pan/tilt and zoom speeds. A new recall replaces an ongoing transition. Pan/tilt and zoom can overlap, and held components release independently. Reviewed setups can use 1×–4× zoom; the default uses small steps at 15 raw units/s. The four Speed levels request 16/24/32/38°/s, subject to the selected native profile. Presets can inherit the app default or save their own transition speed. Tracking uses the current app setting. The separate fast-38-lead4-v1 profile retains 0.5° maximum steps, 0.25° grid, minimum 10ms spacing, 4° readback lead, a 200ms lease and 200ms ramp. These are software bounds, not measured physical motion.

Stream Deck

Use Install Stream Deck plugin or Connect Stream Deck in the app for automatic local pairing. The Recall Preset inspector has one dropdown of actual presets; no token entry is needed. Hold Pan / Tilt or Zoom to move, release to stop scheduling, and use guarded Recenter directly. A Stop key is optional. Camera readiness is managed by the app. Revoke client access in Settings → Integrations and explicitly connect again to re-pair.

Settings, updates and recovery

About shows the installed version and build, project/privacy/support links, copyable app information and update controls.

Settings and Preset tools use compact tabs, cards and contextual hints. Camera controls prepare automatically when you connect, restart or reconnect. There are no setup names or confirmation checkboxes. Press Escape in the app to cancel movement scheduling.

Signed updates install with Update & Restart. Interrupted-update recovery checks the installed app and saved state before reopening and preparing controls. Movement is never replayed. The plugin uses its compatible direct installer separately.

Compatibility and limitations

Apple Silicon · macOS 26+ · standard Osmo Pocket 4 firmware 01.03.14.04. Stream Deck 6.6+ is optional. Validation covers one Mac and an existing macOS account; clean-account and second-Mac installation remain unqualified. Intel, Windows, Linux, other cameras and other firmware are outside this release.

Native DJI ActiveTrack control and battery telemetry are not included. Keep DJI ActiveTrack off on the camera, including while using optional Mac face tracking, and keep travel clear. Mac tracking follows the selected camera feed; OBS must show the controlled Pocket’s full, unmirrored image. A single face is associated by position, not identity. Physical arrival and immediate stop are not guaranteed by readback or completed requests. A finite command accepted by the camera may finish after scheduling stops. Wider-range recovery, full-travel behavior and extended source continuity remain experimental.
