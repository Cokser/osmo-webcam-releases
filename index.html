GOOD TO KNOW

A few useful answers.

Does Osmo Webcam replace OBS?

No. OBS owns direct USB video from the physical camera. The app adds camera controls and uses OBS Virtual Camera for its optional preview.

Does it need Bluetooth or a cloud account?

No Bluetooth connection or app account is required for USB control. Presets and credentials stay in your macOS user account. Update checks use GitHub.

What should I know about the preview?

The preview is experimental. Review clearance for your fixed setup and keep tracking off. Camera readback and completed commands do not establish physical arrival or immediate motor stop. Stop cancels further motion scheduling; a finite command already accepted by the camera may finish.

Where can I report a problem?

Use the public issue tracker. Include versions and the visible error code. Keep tokens, device identifiers and private recordings out of reports. See support for the details to include.
